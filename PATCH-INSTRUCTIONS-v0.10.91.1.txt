------------
1. The next-three-gatherings preview on The Queendom Calendar now includes a compact CLAIM MY SEAT action on each card.
2. Signed-in eligible members register through the existing authenticated Flowtel -> Acuity event-enrollment path. Recurring events preserve the exact occurrence selected.
3. A successfully claimed preview seat becomes: ✓ SEAT CLAIMED · OPEN EVENT.
4. Signed-out visitors see a small two-doorway modal:
   - ALREADY IN THE QUEENDOM? ENTER FLOWTEL
   - NOT YET A MEMBER? JOIN THE QUEENDOM

6. Exact recurring occurrence context is preserved through /client/ login and the v0.10.91 automatic provisioning invitation redirect.
7. The automatic invitation redirect accepts only UUID-shaped event/occurrence ids and always targets the fixed Flowtel /client/ route.
8. Existing event entitlement, ticket, Acuity capacity, idempotency, Womb Magic, and Caddie Magic boundaries are unchanged.
