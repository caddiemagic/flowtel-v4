WHAT CHANGES
- Paid Queendom / Flow FM members no longer manually create a second Flowtel account.
- The signed-out member doorway becomes: Queendom Members | Activate My Flowtel.
- Paid activation asks only for the exact email used at Squarespace checkout.
- Flowtel verifies exact-email Squarespace Contact + PAID mapped membership order fail-closed.
- If the Flowtel Auth identity already exists, the same UUID/password/history are preserved and membership is linked/upgraded in place.
- If no Flowtel Auth identity exists, Supabase Invite User creates it and sends the member an invitation email.
- Migration 078 applies verified membership server-side without lowering existing membership rank, removing Caddie Magic access, replacing history, or reactivating revoked accounts.
- Complimentary Stay signup remains a separate private-password path.
- Owner Administration gains /manager/membership/ to compare verified paid Squarespace membership orders against Flowtel identity/access state and provision selected legacy members.
- Email mismatches are never auto-merged.
