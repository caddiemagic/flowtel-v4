WHAT THIS PATCH DOES
1. Registered recurring Event Rooms show only the exact claimed occurrence being opened.
2. UNCLAIM MY SEAT is available directly inside the registered Event Room and preserves the exact recurring occurrence id.
3. Registered independently claimable events use the simpler copy: ✓ SEAT CLAIMED · OPEN EVENT.
4. The Queendom Calendar shows a compact preview of the next three upcoming gatherings above the month grid.
5. The Lounge defaults directly to MY UPCOMING EVENTS instead of requiring a discovery/My Events toggle.
6. The visible Lounge footer has one VIEW ALL UPCOMING EVENTS doorway to The Queendom Calendar.
7. The Queendom Calendar event dialog also supports Acuity-first UNCLAIM MY SEAT for consistency.
8. Series / Vortex rooms remain multi-session by design.
