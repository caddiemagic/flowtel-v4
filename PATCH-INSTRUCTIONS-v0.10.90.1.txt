PURPOSE
- Fix the browser error `Value must be greater than or equal to 2.` when Owner Event Administration is editing a Single or Recurring event.
- The Series-only controls were marked hidden, but Event Admin CSS forced all form labels to display:grid, overriding their hidden rendering.
- v0.10.90.1 explicitly hides those controls and disables their inputs whenever Event Format is not Series / Vortex.
