WHAT THIS RELEASE DOES
- Makes ONLY /caddie-magic/call-your-caddie/ usable without a Flowtel/Caddie Magic account.
- Keeps the Caddie Mailbox, Show Queue, Studio Mode, mailbox metadata, and recording reads private to The Caddie Master.
- Uses one-path signed private Supabase Storage uploads. No public bucket/read/list policy is added.
- Reuses api/caddie-acuity.js for public-mailbox-init and public-mailbox-finalize so Vercel remains 12/12.
- Adds same-origin checks, honeypot protection, HMAC-hashed connection rate limiting, a 15 MB public upload ceiling, supported MIME checks, a two-hour finalize token, and the existing five-minute limit.
- Restyles Call Your Caddie toward a radio/call-in-show experience.
- Exact HOW IT WORKS copy in this release:
  1. You have a problem or an observation
  2. Leave me a message
  3. Tune in to the Call Your Caddie podcast for my response
- Preserves the optional “Please do not use my name in the podcast” request.
