- Folds the unshipped v0.10.89.1 login/session isolation repair into this deployment.

- Makes Acuity-linked single/recurring seat confirmation Acuity-first and idempotent.
- Keeps Acuity confirmation/reminder emails enabled.
- Adds the universal member reminder doorway: /queendom-events/?enter=1

- Adds append-oriented event attendance snapshots and the four-chamber host Event Flow Map.
- Lets host/co-host/Owner open and refresh the Event Flow Map without claiming an attendee seat or creating an Acuity appointment for themselves.
- Adds automatic New Moon / Half Full Moon / Full Moon / Half New Moon calendar markers and owner lunar context.
- Preserves event visibility != membership != payment != registration != private-room access.
